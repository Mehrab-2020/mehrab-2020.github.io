💡 Why Choose ESP32?

The ESP32 by Espressif is a game-changer for makers and engineers. Unlike standard Arduino boards, ESP32 development boards feature built-in Wi-Fi + Bluetooth (Classic and BLE), making them the top choice for modern IoT applications.

Equipped with the powerful ESP-WROOM-32 module, an ESP32 DevKit provides numerous GPIO pins, capacitive touch sensors, and dual-core processing power. This makes it incredibly versatile for everything from simple home automation to complex robotics applications.

  • ✅ Built-in Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connectivity
  • ✅ Dual-core processor with high clock speed
  • ✅ Extensive GPIO for sensors and actuators
  • ✅ Fully compatible with the Arduino IDE
  • ✅ Perfect for low-power and battery-operated projects
