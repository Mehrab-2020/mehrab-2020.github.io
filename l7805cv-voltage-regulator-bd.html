L7805CV Voltage Regulator

৳ 320

The L7805CV is a classic three-terminal 5V positive voltage regulator. If your project runs on a 9V or 12V battery and you need to power 5V logic ICs or sensors reliably without overheating, this IC is perfectly suited.

Key Features:

  • Output Voltage: 5V
  • Maximum Output Current: 1.5A
  • Internal Thermal Overload Protection
  • Internal Short Circuit Current Limiting
  • TO-220 Package
